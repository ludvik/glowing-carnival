# Repository Working Rules

## Decision Log

During development, maintain a markdown decision log that captures key discussion
outcomes for later presentation preparation.

- Write the log to `docs/decision-log.md`.
- Update it whenever we settle a meaningful methodology, architecture, product,
  evaluation, cost, latency, operational, or scope tradeoff.
- Keep entries concise and dated.
- Record the decision, the reasoning, rejected alternatives when useful, and any
  known limitations or follow-up questions.
- Do not record routine implementation details unless they affect how the work
  should be explained in the final review.
